Introduction

Reese & Honey Cafe

Are you a coffee and pastry lover? Reese & Honey Cafe is the one for you! We are a cozy local cafe located in downtown Walnut Creek off of North Broadway Street. We opened up this past month and are excited to meet and chat with the community. We want to serve as a third space for people to connect and enjoy quality time. We offer a variety of specialty drinks and pastries in a welcoming envrionment open to anyone and everyone.

Target audience

Reese & Honey Cafe targets morning enthusiasts and people seeking a comfortable environment to enjoy quality beverages paired with a savory or sweet pastry.

The primary goal of the target audience is to find a reliable source for delicious beverages and pastries while enjoying a relaxing atmosphere.
