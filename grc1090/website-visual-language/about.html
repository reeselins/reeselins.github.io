Are you a coffee and pastry lover? Reese & Honey Cafe is the one for you! We are a cozy local cafe located in downtown Walnut Creek off of North Broadway Street. We opened up this past month and are excited to meet and chat with the community. We want to serve as a third space for people to connect and enjoy quality time. We offer a variety of specialty drinks and pastries in a welcoming envrionment open to anyone and everyone.

Reese & Honey Cafe targets morning enthusiasts and people seeking a comfortable environment to enjoy quality beverages paired with a savory or sweet pastry.

Reese & Honey Cafe takes pride in offering a thoughtfully curated menu made to brighten anyone's morning. From handcrafted lattes and seasonal specialty drinks to freshly baked croissants, muffins, and other delicious pastries, there is something for everyone to enjoy. We strive to use quality ingredients and bring creativity into every item we serve, making each visit a memorable experience.

Beyond serving great coffee and pastries, Reese & Honey Cafe hopes to become a familiar gathering spot within the Walnut Creek community. Whether you are stopping by for your morning coffee, catching up with a friend, getting some work done, or simply taking a moment for yourself, our doors are always open. We believe the best moments are often shared over a good cup of coffee, and we look forward to creating those moments with our customers.
